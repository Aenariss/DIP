## utils/webdrivers

You can put your webdrivers here to keep them organized and in one place. 
Afterward, just specify the correct path to the desired webdriver in ``content-blocking/config.py``.

#### Folder contents:

Files:
- ``chromedriver_134.exe`` -- Chromedriver 134, downloaded from https://googlechromelabs.github.io/chrome-for-testing/known-good-versions-with-downloads.json
